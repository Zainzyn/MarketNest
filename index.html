🎓 Learn to Trade

Master the fundamentals before risking real money

📈

What is a Stock?

A stock represents fractional ownership in a company. When you buy shares, you own a tiny piece of that business and can profit from its growth and dividends.

🟢

When to BUY

Look for RSI below 30 (oversold), a Golden Cross (SMA10 crossing above SMA30), and strong fundamentals. Don't chase pumps — wait for pullbacks to value zones.

🔴

When to SELL

Sell when RSI exceeds 70 (overbought), a Death Cross forms, or your profit target is hit. A stop-loss should trigger before losses get out of hand. Discipline beats emotion.

📉

RSI Explained

The Relative Strength Index (0–100) measures price momentum. Below 30 = oversold (potential buy opportunity). Above 70 = overbought (potential sell signal). 50 is neutral.

〰️

Moving Averages

SMA smooths price noise over time. A Golden Cross (SMA10 above SMA30) signals bullish momentum. A Death Cross (SMA10 below SMA30) signals bearish momentum.

🛡️

Risk Management

Never risk more than 2% of your portfolio on a single trade. Use stop-losses religiously. Diversify across sectors. Surviving long enough to let compounding work is the goal.
